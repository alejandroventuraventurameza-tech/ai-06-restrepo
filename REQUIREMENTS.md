## 5. This week's trap (issue §5)

> Ask the model: **does automation necessarily reduce wages and the labour share in this model?** The answer that comes back will probably be flatter than the paper's.

> Then work out for yourself which forces push in which direction, and what has to be true for one to dominate. The interesting content is in the conditions, not in the headline. If you can state the condition under which automation *raises* wages, you have understood the model.

> The Lean exercise is part of that evaluation: formal verification is useful precisely because it forces every quantifier, domain restriction, and inequality to be made explicit.

- [ ] Ask the question to a model **cold** and paste the raw answer in `prompts.md`.
- [ ] Our own answer: forces identified, **sign of each**, and the explicit condition under which automation **raises** wages — separately for wages, the labour share and employment, and separately for each regime and horizon the paper distinguishes.
- [ ] Verdict on the model's answer (goes to presentation item 5).
