## Defense questions

- Why is an increase in I not necessarily actual automation? In the free regime the endogenous cost cutoff is already below I.
- Why can W rise while employment and the labor share fall? The productivity gain competes with displacement in the wage, but the latter lowers relative labor demand in the adopted-automation regime.
- Why does the printed NBER threshold fail? The bound makes one branch vacuous and a nonempty admissible neighborhood contradicts the other. AER has corrected its direction.
- What is new in the November work? A conditional single-crossing proof under isoelastic supply, not uniqueness for arbitrary labor supply; independent formal and numerical checks.
- What does successful AML compilation mean? The historical endpoint still contains one sorry. The independent theorems have their own assumptions and axiom audit; neither substitutes for the other.
- Why distinguish long-run regimes? Proposition 5's wage increase concerns the interior constrained BGP. In the interior free BGP, marginal automation leaves effective wages and labor market outcomes unchanged.
